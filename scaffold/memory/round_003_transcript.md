- **S14: callback request.** The caller complains about being called at night and then says "call me due the date" (meaning "call me during the day"). The agent treats this as gibberish instead of a callback request.

**The edit.**
1. Before using a "not engaging" line, scan all earlier agent lines, not just the latest. If "I'm having trouble understanding." appears anywhere, close with "I'm unable to assist at this time. Goodbye" and call `end_call` in the same response. TV/radio/broadcast audio is added to the not-engaging triggers.
2. If a caller objects to the time of the call and names a better time, even in garbled words, that's a callback request. The agent says "Sure, I will arrange a callback, goodbye" and calls `end_call` without asking them to confirm.

**Why the side-effect risk is low.**
