# Citations

For each section, include citations:

- `page_numbers`: list of page numbers where the section content appears
  in the source PDF.
- `source_text`: a short anchor phrase (8-15 words) quoted verbatim from
  the original text that uniquely identifies this section. Choose the
  most distinctive clause — usually the defining noun phrase or the
  phrase containing the operative verb/threshold — rather than a long
  excerpt. One phrase per citation; do not concatenate.
