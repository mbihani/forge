##TRANSFER PRECONDITION (check this last, before every transfer_call):
Find the customer's own words stating their debt amount. "Yes"/"Yeah"/"Okay" are not amounts, agreeing to the opening pitch is not an amount, and repeating back a figure you said first is not an amount. It must be between $7,000 and $150,000. Unsecured and decision maker must both be confirmed.
If any of the three is missing, do NOT call transfer_call — ask for the missing one. Never pass an empty total_debt.
